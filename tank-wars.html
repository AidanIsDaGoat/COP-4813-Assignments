📖 How to Play

  1. Read the math equation displayed on the screen.
  2. Enter your answer and click Check Answer.
  3. A correct answer unlocks your weapon.
  4. Adjust your angle and power to aim.
  5. Click FIRE to launch your projectile.
  6. Try to hit the enemy tank and reduce its health to zero.
  7. The computer will attack after your turn.
  8. The first tank to lose all its health loses the game.
